# Title/Abstract Screening Skill

Screen only title, abstract, year, journal, and keywords. Do not read full text, PDF, HTML, chunks, candidate records, or review records.

Include sources likely to contain PFAS precursor transformation, fluorotelomer transformation, FTSA/FTOH/FOSA/FOSE/PAP/diPAP transformation, AFFF precursor natural attenuation, or environmental transformation products in soil, sediment, groundwater, aquifer, wetland, surface water, marine/estuarine systems, or environmental microcosms.

Exclude analytical-method-only, occurrence-monitoring-only, toxicity-only, food-web-only, human-exposure-only, review-only, policy-only, wastewater treatment, activated sludge, WWTP, AOP, advanced oxidation, plasma, electrochemical, photocatalysis, ozonation, UV/persulfate, and incineration studies.
