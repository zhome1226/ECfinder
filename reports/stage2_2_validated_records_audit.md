## clean_stage2_2_001: 6:2 fluorotelomer sulfonic acid -> 6:2 fluorotelomer unsaturated carboxylic acid

- DOI: 10.1021/acs.est.2c01867
- evidence_tier: probable_product
- setting_type: soil_microcosm_from_field_sample
- environment_matrix: sandy soil and rhizosphere sandy soil
- evidence_quote: Four stable transformation products were detected. These metabolites were 6:2 fluorotelomer unsaturated carboxylic acid (6:2 FTUCA), perfluoropentanoic acid (PFPeA), perfluorohexanoic acid (PFHxA), and perfluoroheptanoic acid (PFHpA), which accounted for 0.5, 0.3, 0.2, and 0.1% of the initial applied 6:2 FTSA, respectively.
- why_accepted: Primary study evidence links the PFAS precursor to a named product in soil or soil-derived environmental microbiome conditions.
- limitations: Primary open full text reports 6:2 FTSA decrease and named stable transformation products in sulfur-limited soil microcosms.
- manual_confirmation_needed: False
