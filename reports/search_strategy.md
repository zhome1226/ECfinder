# Search Strategy

Stage 1 searches target PFAS transformation pathways and transformation products under natural environmental conditions.

## Source Order

1. CrossRef, PubMed, and OpenAlex-compatible metadata.
2. Semantic Scholar or publisher metadata when structured primary sources are insufficient.
3. Manual Google Scholar, Scopus, or Web of Science checks only as last resort and with explicit limitations.

## Core Query Families

- PFAS transformation/degradation in environmental matrices.
- PFAS precursor conversion to terminal PFAS and named products.
- Natural attenuation and environmentally realistic microcosms.
- Phototransformation and sunlight-driven pathways.
- Wastewater, leachate, sludge, and biosolids where environmental relevance is explicit.

## Deduplication

Use normalized DOI as the primary key. If DOI is absent, use normalized title plus first author with high title similarity.
