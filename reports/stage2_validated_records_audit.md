# Stage 2 Validated Records Audit

All 4 records currently come from one primary study: `10.1021/acs.estlett.8b00148`, *Biotransformation of AFFF Component 6:2 Fluorotelomer Thioether Amido Sulfonate Generates 6:2 Fluorotelomer Thioether Carboxylate under Sulfate-Reducing Conditions*.

These records show that the pipeline has found its first natural-environment / environmental-microcosm evidence, but they do not mean the PFAS natural-transformation database is broadly covered. Stage 2.2 must keep targeting other parent classes and environmental media.

## Record 1: 6:2 fluorotelomer thioether amido sulfonate -> 6:2 fluorotelomer thioether propionate

- DOI: 10.1021/acs.estlett.8b00148
- setting_type: soil_microcosm_from_field_sample
- redox_condition: sulfate-reducing; anaerobic; N2/CO2 headspace; sulfate electron acceptor
- evidence_quote: These analyses demonstrated that 6:2 FtTAoS was transformed primarily to a stable polyfluoroalkyl compound, 6:2 fluorotelomer thioether propionate (6:2 FtTP).
- evidence_tier: confirmed_product
- review_status: validated_high_confidence
- why_accepted: Primary-source evidence links the PFAS precursor to a named product in environmental-solids microcosms under sulfate-reducing conditions.
- limitations: Product assignment is standard-confirmed and suitable as core evidence.
